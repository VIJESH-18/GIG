GigVerse Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Compliance Notice: You can request the deletion of your GigVerse account and associated personal data at any time. You do not need to have the GigVerse app installed to make this request.

What data gets deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently removed from our systems (including local databases if applicable):

  • Your profile information (Name, Email, Phone, Location)
  • Government ID and verification records
  • Projects you have posted (if you are a client)
  • Proposals and bids you have submitted
  • Chat messages and collaboration history

Note: Escrow and payment records required for financial compliance and taxation purposes may be retained as legally required before being fully expunged.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

If you have the GigVerse app installed, you can delete your account instantly:

1. Open the GigVerse app and log in.
2. Navigate to the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
4. Tap the red Delete Account button.
5. Your account and local data will be permanently wiped immediately.

Method 2: External Deletion Request (Manual Processing)

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request manual deletion of your account. Because GigVerse currently operates without a centralized backend server, any data stored remotely (such as cached Gemini API request logs, if any) or account registrations will be manually purged by our team.

To initiate a manual deletion request:

1. Click the button below to generate a deletion request email.
2. Ensure you send the email from the same email address associated with your GigVerse account to verify your identity.
3. Our privacy team will process the request and confirm deletion within 7-14 business days.
